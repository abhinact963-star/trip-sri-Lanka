The Itinerary

5 Days, Zero Boring Moments

Each day is a different vibe — city, mountains, coast.

Day 01 · Oct 21

Cyberpunk Colombo

Start your trip in the capital city. Head straight to the Lotus Tower — the tallest structure in South Asia with a 360-degree observation deck, revolving restaurant, and digital exhibition halls. Later, hit Galle Face Green at sunset for street food culture.

🗼 Lotus Tower🌅 Galle Face🍤 Street Food📸 360° Views
Day 02 · Oct 22

World's Most Scenic Train

Board the famous Kandy to Ella train. Watch tea estates, misty valleys, and waterfalls roll by. Open the windows, play some music, and take those iconic doorway photos while the train winds through the mountains.

🚂 Scenic Train🍃 Tea Estates🌫️ Misty Valleys🚪 Doorway Photos
Day 03 · Oct 23

Nine Arch Bridge & Infinity Pools

Start at the Nine Arch Bridge — a massive stone viaduct hidden in tea trees. Just 2 minutes walk. Afternoon at Ravana Pool Club — infinity pools overlooking the valley. Zero effort, high-tier views.

🌉 Nine Arch🛺 Tuk-Tuk♾️ Infinity Pool🏔️ Valley Views
Day 04 · Oct 24

Southern Coast & Red Cliffs

Head to Coconut Tree Hill in Mirissa — red-soil dome covered in tall palms jutting into the ocean. Go at 5 PM for golden hour. Evening at a beach club with feet in the sand.

🌴 Coconut Hill🏖️ Mirissa🌅 Golden Hour🍽️ Beach Dinner
Day 05 · Oct 25

Galle Fort & Mangrove Safari

Galle Fort — UNESCO World Heritage site. Walk the ramparts. Finish at Madu River for a boat safari through mangrove tunnels, cinnamon island, and fish foot massage on a floating deck.

🏰 Galle Fort🚤 Madu River🌿 Mangroves✈️ Airport

Good to Know

Travel Tips

🌧️

October Weather

Expect afternoon rain. Private van means you just wait it out.

🎫

Book Train Early

Observation Class sells out fast. Book weeks in advance.

📸

Golden Hour

Coconut Hill at 5 PM. Nine Arch when a train passes.

💵

Currency

1 INR = 3.45 LKR. Cards in cities, cash for tuk-tuks.

📱

Get a SIM

Dialog at the airport. ~$5 for 10GB. Essential for maps.

🛺

Tuk-Tuks

Negotiate first or use PickMe app — Uber for Sri Lanka.
